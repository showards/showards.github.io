The project was started in Sacramento and sought to fix a multifaceted marketing issue – the symbiotic relationships between farms, farmer’s markets, and restaurants. Organized by a farmer’s market coordinator, groups of restaurant owners and farmers were assembled to speak with Farm-to-Chef Market and voice their concerns. One goal emerged: allow farm information to be shown on a restaurant’s menu, and a restaurant’s information to be shown beside produce on the farm’s website. It was a way to educate restaurant patrons as to where the ingredients originated, and also help generate more business for farms at farmer’s markets.

Initially a secondary concern, but farmer’s market coordinators needed access to the inventory and yields of farms to manage the bulk purchase and delivery of goods from farms to restaurants. It was realized the result of the project would replicate the supply chain of a business: farms to farmer’s markets and then to restaurants and consumers. It was also recognized the replacement of: a farmer’s market with a retailer, a farm with a vendor, and a restaurant with a service provider, similar results would prevail.
