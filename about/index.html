    About

    There is a wealth of information available on the web. It is often easier to reference other writings than it is to reinvent the wheel, so to speak. The referencing/linking of information is desirable in many ways: appropriate attribution or even simply recognizing other writings of similar content. We often try to compete with the same content that has been written about ad nauseam, which can detract from our brand as disputes may arise over original authorship. Additionally, today, we are in competition with large language models to generate attention-grabbing content.

    This is a static HTML site, which inherently suppresses discussion. At this time there is no comment feature, nor any “dynamic” portions, to bolster discussion. Sometimes the effort, and the lack of ease, make the contacts more genuine and fruitful. Contact, in this way, come from the most fervent readers and individuals. There is an email address that can be used; hopefully I won’t receive too much spam. Please be aware that messages encrypted with PGP1 will hold more weight than those that do not.
